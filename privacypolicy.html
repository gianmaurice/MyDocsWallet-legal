Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Your Data Stays on Your Device

MyDocs Wallet is designed as a local app. All documents, images, notes, links, dates and amounts you enter are stored exclusively on your device. The developer operates no server and has no access to your documents or their contents at any time.

iCloud Sync (Optional, Plus Only)

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is synchronized through your own personal, private iCloud account. This data resides solely in your iCloud storage; the developer has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies. Sync is disabled by default and only runs after you turn it on.

Exchange Rates

To convert amounts between currencies, the app fetches exchange rates from frankfurter.app (data source: European Central Bank). Only the requested currency code is transmitted. No personal data, documents or amounts are sent to this service.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases are processed through Apple’s in-app purchase system. The app uses RevenueCat to manage purchase entitlements. This may involve an anonymous user identifier generated by RevenueCat and your purchase status. No documents or app content are transmitted to RevenueCat. The privacy policies of RevenueCat and Apple apply.

Images and Links You Add

If you add a document icon using an image address, that address — entered by you — is requested in order to download the image. If you open a link you saved, it opens in your device’s browser. In both cases, no content from the app is transmitted.

Notifications

The app schedules local reminders on your device. These are generated entirely on-device and are not sent through any server.

No Analytics, No Advertising, No Tracking

MyDocs Wallet contains no advertising, no behavioural analytics tools, and does not sell data.

Your Rights

Because the developer stores no personal data about you, there is no data held by the developer for you to access, correct or delete. You can delete your locally stored data at any time within the app. For data in your iCloud account, please contact Apple.
